# Home Loan Agreement HL-C-0003 (SYNTHETIC)

## Clause 2 - Loan Details
2.1 Outstanding principal as on statement date: Rs 60,00,000.
2.2 Equated Monthly Instalment (EMI): Rs 50,000.

## Clause 6 - Default
6.1 Non-payment of three consecutive EMIs shall constitute an event of default.

## Clause 7 - Mandatory Insurance
7.1 The Borrower shall at all times maintain life insurance assigned to the Lender with a sum assured not less than the outstanding principal.
7.2 Failure to maintain the insurance required under Clause 7.1 shall constitute an event of default, and the Lender may recall the entire outstanding amount.
7.3 Any shortfall between the assigned insurance proceeds and the outstanding principal on the Borrower's death remains recoverable from the Borrower's estate and legal heirs.
