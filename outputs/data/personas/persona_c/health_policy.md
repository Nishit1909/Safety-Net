# Family Floater Health Insurance Policy HF-C-0003 (SYNTHETIC)

## Clause 2 - Sum Insured
2.1 The Sum Insured is Rs 10,00,000 per policy year, shared across all four insured members.

## Clause 3 - Coverage
3.1 In-patient hospitalisation, including surgery, is covered up to the Sum Insured.

## Clause 4 - Limits
4.1 Room rent is covered up to Rs 10,000 per day; a standard single room at the network hospital costs Rs 6,000 per day.

## Clause 6 - Exclusions
6.1 Loss of income is not covered.
