# Term Life Insurance Policy TL-C-0003 (SYNTHETIC)

## Clause 3 - Sum Assured
3.1 The Sum Assured payable on death of the Life Assured is Rs 50,00,000.

## Clause 5 - Accidental Disability Income Rider
5.1 On Temporary Total Disability caused by an Accident, the Insurer pays a monthly benefit of Rs 90,000.
5.2 The monthly benefit commences after a waiting period of 30 days from the date of the Accident and is payable for up to 12 months.
5.3 This rider does not cover illness, resignation or termination of employment.

## Clause 6 - Assignment
6.1 This policy has been absolutely assigned to the home-loan lender under loan account HL-C-0003. Any death benefit is paid to the lender, not to the nominee.
