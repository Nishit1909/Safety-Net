# Family Floater Health Insurance Policy HF-A-0001 (SYNTHETIC)

## Clause 2 - Sum Insured
2.1 The Sum Insured is Rs 10,00,000 per policy year, shared across policyholder, spouse and two children.

## Clause 3 - Coverage
3.1 In-patient hospitalisation expenses for a minimum of 24 hours are covered up to the Sum Insured.
3.2 Pre-hospitalisation expenses for 30 days and post-hospitalisation expenses for 60 days are covered.

## Clause 4 - Limits
4.1 Room rent is covered up to 1% of the Sum Insured per day (Rs 10,000 per day).

## Clause 6 - Exclusions
6.1 Loss of income, salary or wages during hospitalisation or recovery is not covered.
6.2 Pre-existing diseases are covered only after 36 months of continuous coverage.
