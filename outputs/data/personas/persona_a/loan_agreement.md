# Home Loan Agreement HL-A-0001 (SYNTHETIC)

## Clause 2 - Loan Details
2.1 Sanctioned amount: Rs 45,00,000.
2.2 Outstanding principal as on statement date: Rs 40,00,000.
2.3 Equated Monthly Instalment (EMI): Rs 35,000.

## Clause 6 - Default
6.1 Non-payment of three consecutive EMIs shall constitute an event of default.
6.2 On default, the Lender may recall the entire outstanding amount and initiate recovery against the mortgaged property.

## Clause 7 - Insurance
7.1 The Borrower is advised, but not required, to maintain life insurance adequate to cover the outstanding loan.
