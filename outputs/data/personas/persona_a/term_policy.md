# Term Life Insurance Policy TL-A-0001 (SYNTHETIC)

## Clause 1 - Definitions
1.1 "Temporary Total Disability" means a disability caused solely by an Accident that prevents the Life Assured from engaging in any gainful occupation, certified by a registered medical practitioner.
1.2 "Accident" means a sudden, unforeseen and involuntary event caused by external, visible and violent means.

## Clause 3 - Sum Assured
3.1 The Sum Assured payable on death of the Life Assured during the policy term is Rs 1,00,00,000 (One Crore), payable to the nominee.
3.2 The policy term is 30 years from the commencement date.

## Clause 4 - Exclusions
4.1 Death due to suicide within 12 months of commencement is excluded.

## Clause 5 - Accidental Disability Income Rider
5.1 This rider is in force and attached to the base policy.
5.2 On Temporary Total Disability arising from an Accident, the Insurer will pay a monthly benefit of Rs 60,000.
5.3 The monthly benefit commences after a waiting period of 90 days from the date of the Accident.
5.4 The monthly benefit is payable for the duration of the Temporary Total Disability, up to a maximum of 24 months.
5.5 This rider does not cover loss of income due to illness, resignation, termination of employment, or any cause other than an Accident.
