# Individual Health Insurance Policy HI-B-0002 (SYNTHETIC)

## Clause 1 - Schedule
1.1 Sum Insured: Rs 15,00,000 per policy year.
1.2 Continuous coverage under this policy as on statement date: 18 months.

## Clause 3 - Coverage
3.1 In-patient hospitalisation expenses, including surgery and ICU charges, are covered up to the Sum Insured.

## Clause 4 - Room Rent and Proportionate Deduction
4.1 Room rent is payable up to 1% of the Sum Insured per day (Rs 15,000 per day).
4.2 If the insured occupies a room with rent higher than the eligible limit, all associated medical expenses (except medicines and consumables) are paid in the same proportion that the eligible room rent bears to the actual room rent.

## Clause 6 - Exclusions and Waiting Periods
6.1 Loss of income is not covered.
6.2 Pre-existing diseases declared at proposal, and any complications arising from them, are covered only after 36 months of continuous coverage.
6.3 Expenses arising from accidental injury are covered from day one with no waiting period.
