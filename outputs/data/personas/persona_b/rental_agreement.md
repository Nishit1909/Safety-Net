# Residential Rental Agreement RA-B-0002 (SYNTHETIC)

## Clause 2 - Rent
2.1 Monthly rent: Rs 20,000, payable by the 5th of each month.
2.2 Security deposit: Rs 60,000, refundable at the end of the tenancy.

## Clause 5 - Termination
5.1 Either party may terminate with two months' notice. No other liability arises on the tenant's death.
